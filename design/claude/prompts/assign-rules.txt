How to answer:
- Answer from what an ordinary observer would actually see, not from the
  wording of a textbook definition. If a defining feature is too small, too
  brief or too far away for an observer to notice, answer as they would see
  it.
- If it genuinely shows two options about equally often, choose both. Never
  choose more than two.
- Give your confidence in each answer: high, medium or low.
- If you cannot choose, leave the choice empty and give the reason:
  not_observable: an observer could not see or know this;
  ambiguous: more than two options fit, or none fits better than the rest;
  unclear_question: the question or its options are unclear, or do not fit;
  unknown: you do not know.
- Answer each question on its own, by its number.
